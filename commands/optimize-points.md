# Optimize my points

Use the Gondola MCP `optimize_loyalty_portfolio` tool (and `get_loyalty_accounts` for current
balances) to analyze the user's loyalty portfolio: **$ARGUMENTS**

Surface where their points are worth the most right now, which balances are best spent soon, and any
high-value redemptions available given what they hold. If a goal is given, tailor the recommendation
to it. Be concrete: name programs, balances, and specific redemption ideas with their cents-per-point.
